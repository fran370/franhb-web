• **[Bain & Company](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bain-and-company/)** has acquired Spanish AI and ML specialist **[PiperLab](https://www.linkedin.com/company/piperlab/?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/piperlab/?originalSubdomain=es)to bolster its services in Europe, the Middle East, and Africa. This acquisition will lead to a new Advanced Analytics and AI hub within Bain's global Advanced Analytics Group. PiperLab's integration aims to meet the growing demand for AI services among Bain's clients, strengthening its AI capabilities, especially with its strategic focus on Spain. PiperLab, established in Madrid in 2015, provides data-driven solutions to enhance business operations and productivity. [1](#footnote-1)

• **[Amadeus](https://www.linkedin.com/company/amadeus/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/amadeus/)announced the acquisition of **[Voxel](https://www.linkedin.com/company/voxelgroup_net/)**, a provider of electronic invoice and B2B payment solutions for travel sellers, hotels, and other travel players. This will support Amadeus’ vision to deliver a smooth travel payment experience. It will also complement Amadeus’ payments business, Outpayce, by enhancing its existing travel sellers’ product suite, while expanding in the hospitality segment. [2](#footnote-2)

• **[Talde](https://www.linkedin.com/company/talde-gesti-n-s.g.e.c.r.-s.a./?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/talde-gesti-n-s.g.e.c.r.-s.a./?originalSubdomain=es)has acquired a majority stake in **[Plymouth Rubber](https://www.linkedin.com/company/plymouth-rubber-europa-s-a/?originalSubdomain=es)**, a Galician company specializing in electrical insulation tape, through its Talde Capital Crecimiento II fund. This marks Talde's fourth operation with this fund, supporting Plymouth Rubber's international growth. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[Laboratorios Cinfa](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cinfa/?originalSubdomain=es)** has expanded its product portfolio by acquiring the gynecological product range from **[Adamed Laboratorios](https://www.linkedin.com/company/adamed-laboratorios/?originalSubdomain=es)**. This strategic move aims to strengthen Cinfa's presence in the gynecological healthcare sector. [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Oreka IT](https://www.linkedin.com/company/oreka-it/?originalSubdomain=es)** has strengthened its offerings for SMEs by acquiring the Catalan company **[Intarex](https://www.linkedin.com/company/intarex/?originalSubdomain=es)**, specializing in SAP Business One technology services. This acquisition adds to Oreka IT's growth, already serving large companies like Mercedes-Benz and PepsiCo. With Intarex's expertise, Oreka IT aims to enhance its services for medium and small companies. This move expands Oreka IT's presence with a new office in Barcelona, joining its locations in Vitoria, Bilbao, Donostia, and Pamplona. [5](#footnote-5)

• France-based **[Groupe Gaïana](https://www.linkedin.com/company/groupe-gaiana/?originalSubdomain=fr)** acquired **[Tipsa](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tipsa-s-l-/?originalSubdomain=es)**, a Spanish software developer for the wine sector. This move, part of Gaïana's external growth strategy supported by its majority shareholder [Meanings Capital Partners](https://www.linkedin.com/company/meanings-capital-partners/), aims to solidify its leadership in European Agrifood software solutions. Tipsa enhances Gaïana's offerings, particularly in vineyard and winery management, complementing previous acquisitions like [Ayanet](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ayanet-tic/?originalSubdomain=es), also in Spain. [6](#footnote-6)

• **[Grupo IAN](https://www.linkedin.com/company/carretilla/?originalSubdomain=es)** (**[Portobello Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/portobello-capital/?originalSubdomain=es)**) has acquired **[Interoliva](https://www.linkedin.com/company/internacional-olivarera-s.a./?originalSubdomain=es)**, a company based in Dos Hermanas (Sevilla) specializing in the production, packaging, and marketing of olives since 1898. This move is part of Grupo IAN's growth strategy, complementing its black olive production with Interoliva's green olives, particularly the gordal and manzanilla varieties. This acquisition aims to enhance their olive business, maintain quality standards, and increase market share. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Puertas Proma](https://www.linkedin.com/company/puertas-proma-s-a-/about/)** has formed a strategic alliance with **[Adapta Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/adapta-capital/about/)** to drive its international growth. This partnership will support Puertas Proma's expansion plans, enhancing its position in the market. [8](#footnote-8)

• **[Seur](https://www.linkedin.com/company/seur/?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/seur/?originalSubdomain=es)has acquired a significant stake in **[Koiki](https://www.linkedin.com/company/koiki/?originalSubdomain=es)**, a company specializing in last-mile delivery based on social and sustainable economy principles. This partnership strengthens Seur's same-day delivery capabilities, aligning with its strategic goals in the delivery sector. [9](#footnote-9)

• **[Jungle](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wejungle/?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wejungle/?originalSubdomain=es)has acquired the digital consulting firm **[Mr. Milú](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mrmiludigital/?originalSubdomain=es)**, marking its first acquisition since 2022. Mr. Milú employs 45 professionals and specializes in digital strategy, further expanding Jungle's capabilities in the digital marketing sector.[10](#footnote-10)
