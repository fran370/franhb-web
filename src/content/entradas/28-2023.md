• **[ACON Investments](https://www.linkedin.com/company/acon-investments/)** Completes Acquisition of Majority Stake in **[Siex 2001](https://www.linkedin.com/company/siex-2001-sl/about/)**. SIEX designs, certifies, manufactures and distributes gas-based fire extinguishing solutions to clients in over 70 countries. [1](#footnote-1)

• **[Aurica](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aurica-capital/)** acquires a 49% stake in **[Alquiler Seguro](https://www.linkedin.com/company/alquiler-seguro/),** company specializing in the management of rental housing, to face its growth plan. [2](#footnote-2)

• **[Randstad](https://www.linkedin.com/company/randstad/)** buys the **[CTC](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupo-ctc/)** services outsourcing group from **[Portobello](https://www.linkedin.com/company/portobello-capital/)** for 80 million. The private equity fund owned the group since 2006. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[Espiga Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/espiga-capital/)** acquires a stake in **[Grupo Abrasivos Manhattan](https://www.linkedin.com/company/abrasivos-manhattan-sa/)**, manufacturer of high value-added precision grinding wheels. [4](#footnote-4)

• The IT consulting company **[Nunsys](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nunsys/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nunsys/)adds another acquisition and reinforces its industrial area with **[Esfera](https://www.linkedin.com/company/esfera-ingenieria/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/esfera-ingenieria/)**[Ingeniería](https://www.linkedin.com/company/esfera-ingenieria/)**. [5](#footnote-5)

• **Stylitics** Acquires **[Wide Eyes](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wide-eyes-technologies/)**, the Advanced Visual AI Solutions Company for Fashion and Retail.[6](#footnote-6)

• **[Dicsa group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dicsaes/)**, the hydraulic and pneumatic connectors and components manufacturer and distributor, joins the British distribution group **[Diploma](https://www.linkedin.com/company/diplomaplc/)**.[7](#footnote-7)

• Dubai’s **[Five Holdings](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fiveglobalholdings/)** takes over **[Pachá](https://www.linkedin.com/company/pacha-group)**’s Ibiza hotels and nightclubs for 320 million euros. [8](#footnote-8)

• **Brokers & Partners** (**[Miura Partners](https://www.linkedin.com/company/miura-partners/)**) joins forces with **[Sabseg Seguros](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sabseg/)** to create Sabseg Group, the leading independent insurance broker in the Iberian market**.** [9](#footnote-9)

• The Galician aluminum group **[Exlabesa](https://www.linkedin.com/company/exlabesa-industy/)** enters France with the purchase of **[Flandria](https://www.linkedin.com/company/flandria-aluminium/)**. [10](#footnote-10)
