• **[HT Médica](https://www.linkedin.com/company/htmedica/)**, a company in the field of imaging diagnosis in Andalusia, Spain, has opened its capital to private equity firm **[Fremman](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fremman)**. This move is part of HT Médica's strategy to consolidate and expand its growth project. [1](#footnote-1)

• **[Palex Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/palex-medical/)** acquires 100% of **[Innovamedica](https://www.linkedin.com/company/innovamedica-s.p.a./)**, an Italian company supplying high-tech solutions to healthcare centres. The transaction was carried out through **Gada** Group, the subsidiary through which Palex has been operating in Italy since 2022. [2](#footnote-2)

• **[Portobello Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/portobello-capital)**, a prominent Spanish private equity firm, has ventured into the vehicle technical inspection (ITV) business in Portugal through its minority fund. The firm acquired a 24% stake in **Controlauto** from **Brisa**, a Portuguese toll road concessionaire. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[Alliance Healthcare](https://www.linkedin.com/company/alliance-healthcare),** a major player in the pharmaceutical distribution industry, has significantly expanded its market position by acquiring **[Centro Farmacéutico](https://www.linkedin.com/company/centro-farmac%C3%A9utico).** This strategic move positions Alliance Healthcare as a closer competitor to Hefame in terms of market share. [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Simplicity Aligners](https://www.linkedin.com/company/simplicity-aligners)**, a company specializing in orthodontic aligners, has expanded its operations in Spain by integrating the activities of **[PerfekteSmil](https://www.linkedin.com/company/perfektesmil)**, a firm focused on similar orthodontic solutions. [5](#footnote-5)

• **[Caser](https://www.linkedin.com/company/caser)**, a significant player in the insurance sector, has diversified its portfolio by entering the funeral services market through the acquisition of **Pompas Fúnebres** de Padrón.[6](#footnote-6)

• **[Housfy](https://www.linkedin.com/company/housfy)**, a real estate platform, has acquired **[Finques Feliu](https://www.linkedin.com/company/finquesfeliu/)**, a traditional real estate agency. This acquisition represents a strategic move for Housfy, blending its tech-driven real estate model with Finques Feliu's established presence in the traditional real estate market. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Westinghouse Electric Company](https://www.linkedin.com/company/westinghouse-electric-company)**, a global player in nuclear technology and services, has acquired 100% ownership of **[Tecnatom](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tecnatom)**, a Spanish engineering company specializing in nuclear plant operation and maintenance. [8](#footnote-8)

• **[Grupo Moure](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupomoure/)**, owner of the low-cost gas stations Autonetoil and the Elefante Azul car wash centers, acquires the Balearic company **[Petroest](https://www.linkedin.com/company/petroest)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/petroest)to consolidate its presence in the islands and maintain strong growth. A few weeks ago Cepsa announced the acquisition of [Ballenoil](https://maweekly.substack.com/p/week-46-2023-in-m-and-a-and-private?utm_source=%2Fsearch%2Fcepsa&utm_medium=reader2). [9](#footnote-9)

• **[Stikets](https://www.linkedin.com/company/stikets)**, a company known for its personalized labels, has acquired the assets of **[Camaloon](https://www.linkedin.com/company/camaloon-world/)**, a Barcelona-based firm specializing in customized promotional products. [10](#footnote-10)
