> 🍺 Este jueves día 26 de octubre, como cada mes, toca “**Cañax**”. Si te apetece charlar de M&A y Private Equity de manera informal, te invitamos a asistir. Apúntate aquí: **[https://lu.ma/canax1023](https://lu.ma/canax1023)**

• **[ABK Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/abk-group/)** has partnered with **[Cleon Capital Advisors](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cleon-capital/)**, who enters the company’s capital as a minority shareholder. Cleon has invested €45 million, mostly through a capital increase, with the aim of supporting ABK Group’s upcoming development and expansion stage. [1](#footnote-1)

• **[Wallbox](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wallbox-chargers/)**, provider of charging and energy management solutions for electric vehicles, announces the acquisition of **[ABL](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ablseit1923/)**. The investment accelerates and strengthens the competitive position of both brands in attractive key markets and customer segments. [2](#footnote-2)

• **[Técnicas Sanjorge (TSJ)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sanjorgefielmaking/?originalSubdomain=es)**, a company specializing in the provision of services and agricultural solutions, is pleased to announce that it has opened its capital to **[Mediterrania Capital Partners](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mediterrania-capital-partners/?original_referer=https%3A%2F%2Fwww%2Egoogle%2Ecom%2F&originalSubdomain=mt)**, along with the Italian investor club **[BIC Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bic-capitalsrl/about/)**. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[ITP Aero](https://www.linkedin.com/company/itpaero/), a [Bain Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bain-capital/?originalSubdomain=es)** portfolio company, through its affiliate ITP Aero US, has signed a definitive agreement to acquire the majority of **[BP Aero](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bp-aero/)**, an Irving (Texas, USA) based leading provider of aircraft engine aftermarket services. [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Corus](https://www.linkedin.com/company/careventures/?originalSubdomain=be)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/careventures/?originalSubdomain=be)strengthens its European bet with the acquisition of **Brunns Dental**. The company, owned by **[Careventures](https://www.linkedin.com/company/careventures/?originalSubdomain=be)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/careventures/?originalSubdomain=be)and **[Quadrum Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/quadrum-investment-fund/)**, continues its international growth with the Danish laboratory after acquiring Byrnes Dental Laboratory in the United Kingdom (2023) and Nordentic in the Scandinavian countries (2022). [5](#footnote-5)

• **[Seidor](https://www.linkedin.com/company/seidor/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/seidor/)acquires a majority stake in the software and technology consulting company for the meat sector **[Delsys](https://www.linkedin.com/company/delsys/)**. [6](#footnote-6)

• **[Ebro](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ebro-official/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ebro-official/)raises 20 million to manufacture electric vehicles at the former Nissan factory. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Studio 100 Media](https://www.linkedin.com/company/studio100-media/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/studio100-media/)Acquires Stake in Tenerife’s **[3Doubles Animation Studio](https://www.linkedin.com/company/3doublesproducciones/?originalSubdomain=es).** [8](#footnote-8)

• The **Bonomi family** (Investindustrial) has acquired a 13.5% stake in the Spanish private debt manager **[Oquendo Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/oquendo-capital/?originalSubdomain=es)**. [9](#footnote-9)

• The Canadian infrastructure fund **[Brookfield](https://www.linkedin.com/company/brookfield-asset-management/)** will acquire a 25% of the capital of **[APK2](https://www.linkedin.com/company/apk2-gestion-de-aparcamientos/?originalSubdomain=es)**, a parking operator, and the Swiss fund **[Partners Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/partners-group/)** 24%. The remaining 51% will remain in the hands of funds managed by **[Firmum Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/firmumcapital/)**. [10](#footnote-10)
