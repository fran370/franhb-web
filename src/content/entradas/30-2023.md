> _Last weekly update until the end of August. Have a great summer_ _🌊⛱️!_

• The British private equity fund **[Apax](https://www.linkedin.com/company/apax-partners/)** has reached an agreement with **[Fremman Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fremman/?originalSubdomain=uk)**, a management company led by the Spanish executive Ricardo de Serdio, to acquire 50% of **[Palex Medical](https://www.linkedin.com/company/palex-medical/)**, a Catalan company that is a leader in the medical equipment distribution market. [1](#footnote-1)

• **[Allfunds](https://www.linkedin.com/company/allfunds-wealthtech/)**, one of the world’s leading B2B WealthTech platforms for the fund industry, offering fully integrated solutions for both fund houses and distributors, and **Iccrea Banca** have signed today a Memorandum of Understanding by which Allfunds will acquire Iccrea Banca’s local paying agent business . [2](#footnote-2)

• **[SIA](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sia-group/?originalSubdomain=es)**, **Indra**'s cybersecurity company, has bought the Spanish company **[ICA Sistemas y Seguridad](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ica-sistemas-y-seguridad/?originalSubdomain=es) (ICASyS)**, from the ICA group, specialized in the design and management of technological infrastructures in secure environments, and with a portfolio of its own cybersecurity solutions. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[Serveo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/serveo-com/)**, empresa participada por el fondo de inversión **[Portobello Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/portobello-capital/?originalSubdomain=es)**, adquiere la actividad de facility management de Sacyr a través de la compra de la compañía **[Sacyr](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sacyr/?originalSubdomain=es) Facilities**. [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Permira](https://www.linkedin.com/company/permira/)** revolutionizes the alternative management sector with the purchase of 40% of **[AltamarCAM](https://www.linkedin.com/company/altamarcam/?originalSubdomain=es)**, the largest independent fund manager in Spain. [5](#footnote-5)

• **[Mapfre](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mapfre/?originalSubdomain=es)**, leader in the Latin American insurance market, has reached an agreement with the Mexican life insurance company **[Insignia Life](https://www.linkedin.com/company/insignia-life)** to acquire 94% of its shares for an initial amount of 86 million euros. [6](#footnote-6)

• **[GED Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ged-capital/?originalSubdomain=es)** integrates **[Snake](https://www.linkedin.com/company/snake-producciones/?originalSubdomain=es) and [Energy Services](https://www.linkedin.com/company/energy-services-lighting/)** with **[Cinelux](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cinelux-sa/?originalSubdomain=es)**, creating Parasol Media, a leading audiovisual services group. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Quarza Inversiones](https://www.linkedin.com/company/quarza-inversiones/about/)**, Cristian Abelló's Private Equity, enters the shareholding of the Catalan manufacturer of nautical vessels **[De Antonio Yachts](https://www.linkedin.com/company/de-antonio-yachts/?originalSubdomain=es)**, which this year will exceed 30 million in sales. [8](#footnote-8)

• **[Oquendo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/oquendo-capital/?originalSubdomain=es)** enters **[HaciendasBio](https://www.linkedin.com/company/haciendasbio?originalSubdomain=es)** after reaching an agreement with the bank and completing its restructuring. Oquendo is already present in several companies in the organic agri-food sector, such as The Natural Fruit Company, Agrupapulpí, Alsur, and Surexport. [9](#footnote-9)

• **[Evolutio](https://www.linkedin.com/company/evolutio-empowering-the-cloud/?originalSubdomain=es)**, compañía participada principalmente por la gestora de capital privado **[Portobello Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/portobello-capital/?originalSubdomain=es)**, ha adquirido la tecnológica española **[Dagram](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dagram/?originalSubdomain=es)**. [10](#footnote-10)
