• **[Cinven](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cinven/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cinven/)agrees to acquire a majority stake in **[Amara NZero](https://www.linkedin.com/company/amaranzero/)** from **[ProA Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/proa-capital/)**.[1](#footnote-1)

• **[Neuraxpharm](https://www.linkedin.com/company/neuraxpharm/)** (**[Permira](https://www.linkedin.com/company/permira/)**) acuerda la venta de **[Inke](https://www.linkedin.com/company/inke/)** a **[Keensight Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/keensight-capital/)**.[2](#footnote-2)

• **[Solum Partners](https://www.linkedin.com/company/solum-partners/)** compra **Greentastic**, el gigante hortofrutícola español de **[Magnum](https://www.linkedin.com/company/magnum-capital-industrial-partners/)**.[3](#footnote-3)

• **[Anco](https://www.linkedin.com/company/anco-technology/)** adquiere **[Crea IT](https://www.linkedin.com/company/crea-it/)** para impulsar su negocio en Madrid.[4](#footnote-4)

• **[Suma Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/suma-capital/)** compra el 90% de Zamora Eco Energías, filial de **[DH Ecoenergías](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dh-ecoenerg%C3%ADas/)**, por 25 millones.[5](#footnote-5)

• Spanish **[Additio App](https://www.linkedin.com/company/additio-for-schools/)** becomes part of the **[Sdui Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sdui/)**.[6](#footnote-6)

• **[Gemba](https://www.linkedin.com/company/navis-cap/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/navis-cap/)supera los 15 millones para comprar pymes y amplía su cartera con la adquisición de **Gines Coll**.[7](#footnote-7)

• **[Grupo Godó](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupo-god%C3%B3-de-comunicaci%C3%B3n/)** adquiere una participación mayoritaria en **[Grupo Experientia S&E](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mktg-spain/)**.[8](#footnote-8)

• **[Keystone Education Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/keystone-education-group/)** has acquired two European-based student athlete placement agencies, **[I-Con Sports](https://www.linkedin.com/company/i-con-sports/)** (Spain) and Future Elite Sports (UK).[9](#footnote-9)

• **[Plexus Tech](https://www.linkedin.com/company/plexus-tech/)** se refuerza con la compra de la consultora **[Brücke](https://www.linkedin.com/company/brucke-by-plexus/).**[10](#footnote-10)
