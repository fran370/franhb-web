> ¡El jueves 27 de julio os esperamos en nuestras **CañaX** mensuales de **Madrid**!
> 
> Despedimos julio y sus 40ºC con unas cañas 🍻 con amigos y colaboradores. Entre las 30-35 personas que nos solemos reunir todos los meses, hay todo tipo de perfiles: inversores, empresarios, directivos, emprendedores, abogados, asesores, etc. —> Si te cuadra, solicita una plaza aquí: [https://lu.ma/canax0723](https://lu.ma/canax0723)

• The Danish **[ISS](https://www.linkedin.com/company/iss-facility-services-a-s/)** expands in Spain through the acquisition of the Extremadura based cleaning services group, **[Fissa](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupo-fissa/)**. [1](#footnote-1)

• **[Portobello Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/portobello-capital/?originalSubdomain=es)** acquires 45% of **[Eurocebollas](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eurocebollas-sl)**, a company based in Alzira, from **[Nazca Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nazcacapital/?originalSubdomain=es)** to boost its international expansion. Eurocebollas specializes in the production of processed onions ready to be used as a cooking ingredient. [2](#footnote-2)

• **[MOIRA Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/moira-capital-partners/?originalSubdomain=es)** and **[INBERGUNE](https://www.linkedin.com/company/inbergune-capital-partners/?originalSubdomain=es)** have acquired 30% of **[Cimico](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cimicobiotechnologies/)**. Cimico is at the forefront of innovation in biotechnological solutions for biological wastewater treatment. [3](#footnote-3)

• **SPEAR Investments** to acquire Barcelona’s electric mobility firm **[QEV Technologies](https://www.linkedin.com/company/qev-technologies/)** in a SPAC deal. [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Logista](https://www.linkedin.com/company/logista/?originalSubdomain=es)** strengthens its presence in the Italian market with the acquisition of **[Gramma Farmaceutici](https://www.linkedin.com/company/gramma-farmaceutici---societa-a-responsabilita-limitata/?originalSubdomain=it)**, a company dedicated to providing logistical services for the pharmaceutical industry. [5](#footnote-5)

• **[Atitlan](https://www.linkedin.com/company/atitlan-grupo)**, the Valencian investment fund is very close to reentering the restaurant sector through **[Tastia Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tastia-group?originalSubdomain=es)**. The Castellon-based company is going through a "delicate" period recently. [6](#footnote-6)

• The Spanish technology company **[Arquimea](https://www.linkedin.com/company/arquimea-group/)** acquires 100% of the American company **[Ecliptic](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ecliptic-enterprises-corp/)**. The US company is a leader in the space sector for optical payloads and avionics. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Talde Private Equity](https://www.linkedin.com/company/talde-gesti-n-s.g.e.c.r.-s.a./?originalSubdomain=es)** invests in **[AVS Added Value Solutions](https://www.linkedin.com/company/addedvaluesolutions/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/addedvaluesolutions/)to drive the Group's strategic plan. AVS is a Group based in Elgoibar (Spain) leader in the design and development of critical and high value-added equipment for the Space market and Big Science. [8](#footnote-8)

• Merger in the Valencian orange industry: **[Guillem Export](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupo-guillem-export/)** and **[Frutas Tono](https://www.linkedin.com/company/frutas-tono/)** create a group worth 160 million euros. Just a few weeks ago, other Valencian companies, Torres y Llusar (backed by MCH Private Equity) and SanLucar Fruit, took a similar step with the integration of Iberian Premium Fruits. [9](#footnote-9)

• The Portuguese group **[Mercurius Health](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mercuriushealth/)** has completed the acquisition of the nuclear medicine specialist **Cadpet**, in a strategic move for the development of the company in Spain. [10](#footnote-10)
