• **[Eurocebollas](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eurocebollas/about/)** and **[Portobello Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/portobello-capital?originalSubdomain=es)** have reached a strategic collaboration agreement with the company **[Solina](https://www.linkedin.com/company/solina-group/?originalSubdomain=es)**, owned by the **[Astorg](https://www.linkedin.com/company/astorg/)** fund, which will result in the entry of the French company with a 7% stake in the capital of the company specialized in the processing of ready-to-use cooked onions. [1](#footnote-1)

• **[Cegid](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cegid/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cegid/)acquires **[REVO](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cegidrevo/)** to drive the digitization of the horeca and retail sectors. REVO is Cegid's fifth acquisition in Spain so far this year. Revo achieved sales of €3 million in 2022. [2](#footnote-2)

• **[Tier1](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tier1-s-l-/)** buys 40% stake in **[Nextt](https://www.linkedin.com/company/next-&-tech-2013-s-l-/)** to expand its restaurant software business. Tier recorded revenues in excess of 18 million euros in 2022, while Nextt reached sales of 2 million euros. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[LVMH](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lvmh/)** invests in Spanish industry and buys Valencian leather supplier **[Verdeveleno](https://www.linkedin.com/company/verdeveleno-s.l./)**. The French luxury conglomerate has bought 55% of the Valencian tannery company through its Metiérs d'Arts division, which specializes in investments in industrial and artisan companies. [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Gambastar](https://www.linkedin.com/company/gambafresh/?originalSubdomain=es)** co-founder José Ignacio Cuevas has sold his 50% stake in the Spain-based prawns and shrimps producer to the Dutch group **Primstar**. Gambastar reported 2022 sales of EUR 106.08m. [5](#footnote-5)

• **[Cropsalsa](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cropsalsa/)**, a Madrid-based manufacturer of frozen and refrigerated sauces acquired 100% of Leon-based sauces producer **[Industrias del Bierzo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ibsabierzo/?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ibsabierzo/?originalSubdomain=es)(Ibsa). Ibsa closed 2022 with net sales of EUR 11.7m. [6](#footnote-6)

• **[AKSA Power Generation](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aksa-power-generation/)**, a company from Turkey, becomes the majority shareholder of **[Dagartech](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dagartech/?originalSubdomain=es)**, a Spain-based company engaged in the design, manufacture and marketing of electric generators. Dagartecha expects to close the year with a turnover of 27 million euros, 30% more than in 2022. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[AGUNSA](https://www.linkedin.com/company/agunsa/)**, a logistics services Chilean multinational acquires 75% of **[ESBO](https://www.linkedin.com/company/esbo-logistic-systems/?originalSubdomain=es)**, in order to expand its presence in the Spanish market, consolidating their respective customer portfolios and incorporating technology and automated processes to their operations. [8](#footnote-8)

• **[Grupo Castilla](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupo-castilla/)**, a Human Resources services and technological solutions company, strengthens its services area with the integration of the Navarre-based company **[Oniria Consulting](https://www.linkedin.com/company/oniria-consulting/?originalSubdomain=es)**, adding a specialized team with more than 25 years of experience in personnel selection, as well as in people and training consulting. [9](#footnote-9)

• **[Aciturri](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aciturri/?originalSubdomain=es) Aeroengines**, a Spanish supplier of engine components integrated in Aciturri, has formalized the acquisition of **[GOAM Industrie](https://www.linkedin.com/company/goam-industrie/)**, a company in the sector located near Casablanca, Morocco. [10](#footnote-10)
