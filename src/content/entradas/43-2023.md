• **[Izertis](https://www.linkedin.com/company/izertis/?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/izertis/?originalSubdomain=es)acquires **[Keifi](https://www.linkedin.com/company/keifi-technologies/?originalSubdomain=es)**, a consultancy firm focused on the development of technological solutions in the field of capital markets (brokerage, settlement and custody), financial asset distribution platforms and digital transformation projects. If you want to know more about Izertis, [in this podcast we discussed the origin of the company and its M&A strategy](https://empresax.com/blog/metamorfosis-digital-con-pablo-martin-izertis/) with Pablo Martín. [1](#footnote-1)

• **[Hitachi Energy](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hitachienergy/?originalSubdomain=ch)** acquires Spain based **[eks Energy](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eks-energy)**, a leading supplier of power electronics and energy management solutions for storage and renewables integration. [2](#footnote-2)

• The Spanish startup **[Signalit](https://www.linkedin.com/company/signalitio?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/signalitio?originalSubdomain=es)has acquired **[Noister](https://www.linkedin.com/company/signalitio?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/signalitio?originalSubdomain=es)to develop a real-time observability platform that will allow companies to have "full control" over their associated data and costs. Following this acquisition, the new combined company will be called **Onum**. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[Surexport](https://www.linkedin.com/company/surexport?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/surexport?originalSubdomain=es)Compañía Agraria (“Surexport”), an **[Alantra](https://www.linkedin.com/company/alantra)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/alantra)Private Equity portfolio company and Europe’s leading independent producer and distributor of fresh berries, has successfully completed three highly strategic add-on acquisitions: Portuguese raspberry producer Solana, Moroccan blueberry producer AG Group, and Spanish organic berry producer Flor de Doñana. [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Mahou San Miguel](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mahou-san-miguel/?originalSubdomain=es)** has acquired a 40% stake in the **[Servitel Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupo-servitel?originalSubdomain=es)**, a company with which there had been a long time collaboration for the supply of equipment to bars and restaurants. [5](#footnote-5)

• **[Smithfield](https://www.linkedin.com/company/smithfield-foods)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/smithfield-foods)takes control of **[Argal](https://www.linkedin.com/company/argal-alimentacion/?originalSubdomain=es)**, a historic meat company borned in 1914 in Pamplona. This year the company forecasts 440 million euros in revenue. Smithfield Foods is an American group with a historical tradition in the sector. It employs nearly 60,000 people distributed in seven countries around the world. The owner of the brand is the Chinese group **WH** [6](#footnote-6)

• **[Sabseg Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sabseg-group/?originalSubdomain=es),** an insurance brokerage services entity controlled by Spain-based private equity firm **[Miura Partners](https://www.linkedin.com/company/miura-partners/?originalSubdomain=es)**, acquired several brokerage firms in Madrid. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[TheProject](https://www.linkedin.com/company/theprojectbcn/?originalSubdomain=es)**, a company engaged in the organization of musical events, acquired 80% stake of Éxits Espectacles from **[Éxits Managers & Produccions](https://www.linkedin.com/company/exits/?originalSubdomain=es).** [8](#footnote-8)

• French group **[Riou Glass](https://www.linkedin.com/company/riouglass/)** acquires 100% of Catalan company **[Vidresif](https://www.linkedin.com/company/vidresif/?originalSubdomain=es)**. Based in Porqueres (Pla de l'Estany) Vidresif specializes in the manufacture of technical glass. [9](#footnote-9)

• **[Hijos de Rivera](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hijosderivera?originalSubdomain=es)** acquires **[Soul K](https://www.linkedin.com/company/soulk?originalSubdomain=es)**, Andalusian kombucha manufacturing company. The Galician group reinforces its alcohol-free beverage business with this acquisition. [10](#footnote-10)
