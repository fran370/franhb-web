• [Grenergy Renovables](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grenergyrenovables/) bolsters business in US by taking full control of [Sofos Harbert Renewable Energy](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sofos-harbert/). [2](#footnote-2)  
• [SAPA](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sapa-superior-auto-parts-always/) compra una participación mayoritaria en [Hispamoldes](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupo-hispamoldes/), participada de [Quarza Inversiones](https://www.linkedin.com/company/quarza-inversiones/). [3](#footnote-3)  
• [itsme.](https://www.linkedin.com/company/itsme-b-v/) adquiere una participación mayoritaria en [Robotplus](https://www.linkedin.com/company/robotplus-s-l-/). [4](#footnote-4)  
• [Impress](https://www.linkedin.com/company/impress-orthodontincs/) compra la empresa de alineadores dentales [Diamond Whites](https://www.linkedin.com/company/diamond-whites/).[5](#footnote-5)  
• [MONDRAGON](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mondragon-corporation/) compra [MAEL COCCION](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mael-coccion-sl/), el proveedor de Balay, Siemens y Bosch en España. [6](#footnote-6)  
• El grupo austriaco [Glatz](https://www.linkedin.com/company/glatz-gmbh-&-co/) entra en España con la compra de [CONSERVAS OLASAGASTI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/conservas-olasagasti/). [7](#footnote-7)  
• [Viessmann](https://www.linkedin.com/company/viessmann/), uno de los principales grupos europeos de calefacción entra en el accionariado de [KEYTER](https://www.linkedin.com/company/keyter/). [8](#footnote-8)  
• [Uptime Institute](https://www.linkedin.com/company/uptime-institute/) ([Dominus Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dominus-capital/)) Acquires Spain based [LEET Security](https://www.linkedin.com/company/leet-security-sl/). [9](#footnote-9)  
• [NIMGenetics](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nimgenetics/) se expande en Europa y adquiere [Gene Predictis SA](https://www.linkedin.com/company/gene-predictis-sa/about/). [10](#footnote-10)
