• **[Eiffage Energía Sistema](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eiffageenergiasistemas/posts/)**s has acquired Mallorca-based Comercial Frío Balear (CFB) to bolster its presence in the Balearic Islands and expand its industrial refrigeration capabilities. Founded in 1989, CFB specializes in commercial refrigeration, HVAC, and ventilation for the food, retail, and hospitality sectors. The integration will strengthen Eiffage’s regional network, facilitate technical knowledge sharing across its Spanish teams, and allow the group to offer broader, more specialized energy services to clients.[1](#footnote-1)

• **[Eysa](https://www.linkedin.com/company/estacionamientos-y-servicios-s-a-u/posts/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/estacionamientos-y-servicios-s-a-u/posts/)has acquired Sweden’s **[ParkTrade](https://www.linkedin.com/company/parktrade-europe-ab/home/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/parktrade-europe-ab/home/)from UK-based Marston Holdings to expand its European tolling and mobility-payment business. Founded in 2005 and based in Stockholm, ParkTrade uses EPASS24 to identify and collect congestion charges from foreign vehicles in Stockholm and Gothenburg, and ParkiaPay to process cross-border parking fines, including in Amsterdam. The acquisition complements Eysa subsidiary Tecsidel’s tolling technology by adding capabilities to identify vehicle owners, notify them of charges, and collect payments across borders. It is Eysa’s third acquisition since Tikehau Capital became its reference shareholder in August 2025, following Aluvisa’s traffic-management unit and corporate mobility platform Joinup. ParkTrade reported €7.7 million in revenue and approximately €3.3 million in net profit in 2025. Eysa operates in more than 60 countries and 275 cities.[2](#footnote-2)

• **[TAKRAF](https://www.linkedin.com/company/takraf-group/home/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/takraf-group/home/)Group completed its acquisition of Madrid-based **[FSI FILTRACIÓN](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fsi-filtracion/home/)**, strengthening DELKOR’s liquid/solid separation and filtration portfolio. Founded in 1989, FSI brings more than 35 years of experience and over 300 chamber and membrane filter press references, serving a range of industrial sectors. Its expertise complements DELKOR’s capabilities, while TAKRAF’s international network offers opportunities to expand the combined offering into new markets. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[Sorigué](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sorigue/home/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sorigue/home/)has acquired **[Eurocatalana Obres i Serveis](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eurocatalana-obres-i-serveis-s-l-/home/)** to expand its urban services offering and strengthen its expertise in the restoration and rehabilitation of historic buildings. The acquisition adds heritage conservation to Sorigué’s Espacios urbanos business line, which already provides maintenance and renovation services for buildings, public facilities, and streets. Eurocatalana’s notable projects include restoration work at Park Güell, Barcelona City Hall, the Hospital de Sant Pau, and the Monastery of Pedralbes. The deal supports Sorigué’s strategy of sustainable growth and its goal of offering clients comprehensive services throughout the lifecycle of their assets.[4](#footnote-4)

• [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aperitivos-y-extrusionados-s-a-/posts/)**[Capitole](https://www.linkedin.com/company/capitole-consulting/posts/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/capitole-consulting/posts/)and **[Profile](https://www.linkedin.com/company/profile-software)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/profile-software)will merge under the Capitole brand, creating a company with more than 1,800 professionals, including over 300 from Profile. The integration formalizes their collaboration within the Smart4Engineering group and gives clients a single contact across the full lifecycle of their systems. Profile contributes expertise in software architecture, DevOps, AI, and the insurance sector, alongside experience in several other industries. [5](#footnote-5)

• **[Grupo DIA](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dia-grupo/home/)** has finalized its strategic transformation by selling its Dia Argentina subsidiary to Dorinka S.R.L. (**[Grupo de Narváez](https://www.linkedin.com/company/gdn-ar/home/)**) for approximately €48 million. Following previous exits from Portugal, Brazil, and large-format stores, this divestment allows DIA to focus exclusively on its highly profitable neighborhood supermarket model in Spain. The sale eliminates the need for future capital injections into the volatile Argentine market and immediately improves the group’s proforma financial metrics, including net sales growth and EBITDA margins. The proceeds will be used to accelerate organic expansion across DIA’s 2,400+ Spanish stores and strengthen its credit profile ahead of refinancing. [6](#footnote-6)

• [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/optiogroup/home/)**[Optio Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/optiogroup/home/)** has agreed to acquire **[Names Europe](https://www.linkedin.com/company/names-agencia-de-suscripci-n/home/)**, a Madrid-based marine underwriting agency, for an undisclosed sum. Founded in 2008, Names Europe specialises in technically complex marine and transportation risks and has an experienced team. The proposed acquisition would strengthen Optio’s marine capabilities and establish a specialist platform in Spain, supporting the group’s continued European expansion. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Customs Support Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/customs-support/posts/)** (CSG), NL-based independent customs services provider, has acquired Barcelona-based **[TEN Aduanas](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ten-aduanas/home/)**, an AEO-certified company. This strategic acquisition significantly strengthens CSG’s presence in Spain, expanding its geographical coverage along the Mediterranean coast and adding specialized capabilities in transit operations (T1), cruise, and offshore customs solutions. Both companies will maintain their current offices to ensure seamless service continuity. This follows CSG’s previous Spanish acquisitions in Algeciras (2022) and Madrid (2025). [8](#footnote-8)

• [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/indragroupglobal/posts/)**[Hormipresa](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hormipresa/home/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hormipresa/home/)has acquired Prefabricados Hernández’s factory in Utrillas, making it the company’s fourth production site in Spain. The 35,000-square-meter facility includes 10,000 square meters of production space. Planned investment will integrate the plant into Hormipresa’s operations and gradually double its capacity to 60,000 cubic meters of precast concrete annually. The expansion will support projects in public housing, residential construction, logistics, industry, public works, and data centers. Across its facilities, Hormipresa will exceed 400,000 square meters and produce more than 300,000 cubic meters of precast concrete per year. Its workforce will grow to over 550 employees. [9](#footnote-9)

• **[Eurofesa](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eurofesa/posts/)**, part of Presto Group, has incorporated the business and team of Arrper Instalaciones Contra Incendios, a fire protection installation and maintenance company based in Tenerife. Founded in 2010, Arrper serves customers on Tenerife and Gran Canaria, with maintenance providing a strong base of recurring business. The acquisition gives Eurofesa its own established platform in the Canary Islands, enabling it to serve national customers with local teams and consistent service across Spain. Arrper’s owners describe joining Eurofesa and Presto as a natural continuation of their long-standing collaboration, offering customers access to a broader range of services while retaining local presence.[10](#footnote-10)
