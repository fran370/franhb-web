• [Alive Business Consulting](https://www.linkedin.com/company/alive-business-consulting/) pone foco en Portugal con la adquisición de [Add Value Consulting Services](https://www.linkedin.com/company/add-value-consulting-services/).[1](#footnote-1)

• [Seresco](https://www.linkedin.com/company/seresco/) compra el 76,87% de la empresa portuguesa [ELO Solutions](https://www.linkedin.com/company/elo---sistemas-de-informa-o-lda/).[2](#footnote-2)

• [Talenom](https://www.linkedin.com/company/talenom/) expands its operations in Spain by acquiring [BV Asesores](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bv-asesores/).[3](#footnote-3)

• [PIB Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/pib-group/) ([Apax](https://www.linkedin.com/company/apax-partners/) y [The Carlyle Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-carlyle-group/)) suma el séptimo bróker en España con la compra de **#Privat**.[4](#footnote-4)

• [Enex Technologies](https://www.linkedin.com/company/enextechnologies/) has acquired [EOS Refrigeration Equipment, S.L](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eos-refrigeration-equipment-s-l-/).[5](#footnote-5)

• [FIATC Seguros](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fiatc-seguros/) compra una parte de la cartera de Salud de **#Murimar**.[6](#footnote-6)

• El search fund [Emptio Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/emptiocapital/) compra el líder en piezas de automoción español [Recambiofacil](https://www.linkedin.com/company/recambiofacil/).[7](#footnote-7)

• [IMAP Albia Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/albia-capital-partners/mycompany/) asesora a [Groupe Kardham](https://www.linkedin.com/company/groupe-kardham/) en su entrada en el mercado español con la adquisición de [Gabiteco](https://www.linkedin.com/company/gabiteco/).[8](#footnote-8)

• El [Grupo Editorial Tirant lo Blanch](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tirant-lo-blanch-online/) llega a Costa Rica con la compra de la empresa [Master Lex](https://www.linkedin.com/company/master-lex/about/).[9](#footnote-9)

• [Avatel Telecom](https://www.linkedin.com/company/avateltelecom/) compra la firma de Alicante [Telitec Connections SL](https://www.linkedin.com/company/avateltelecom/)[10](#footnote-10)
