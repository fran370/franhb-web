• **[ACS Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupo-acs/posts/)** has agreed, through Dragados, to acquire 100% of **[Voltan](https://www.linkedin.com/company/voltangroup/home/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/voltangroup/home/)Group from majority shareholder **[Henko Partners](https://www.linkedin.com/company/henko-partners/posts/)**. The acquisition expands ACS’s capabilities in energy and power infrastructure, including transmission and distribution, electromechanical installations, commissioning, operations and maintenance. Voltan’s expertise will support projects across ACS businesses and markets, including power generation, nuclear projects and digital infrastructure. Founded in 2021, Voltan brings more than 45 years of combined experience, employs over 1,200 people and operates in several countries. It expects to generate approximately €150 million in revenue in 2026. [1](#footnote-1)

• **[Abac Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/abac-capital/posts/)** has acquired significant stakes in Spanish industrial automation firms **[Ansotec Soluciones Tecnológicas](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ansotec-soluciones-tecnol%C3%B3gicas-sl/posts/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ansotec-soluciones-tecnol%C3%B3gicas-sl/posts/)and Bigmon Energía, launching a platform intended to serve industrial clients nationwide and expand through further acquisitions. Ansotec designs and integrates automation, control, and industrial software solutions; Bigmon manufactures electrical panels and integrates control and automation systems. Both companies’ founders will continue managing their businesses. [2](#footnote-2)

• **[Truyol](https://www.linkedin.com/company/truyol/home/)**, a Spanish digital printing company based in Alcorcón, has joined **[WIRmachenDRUCK](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wirmachendruck-gmbh/home/)**, part of the international Cimpress group. The integration supports Truyol’s international expansion and may lead to joint projects across Cimpress’s European network. Truyol will retain its corporate identity, current management, team, and operations, with CEO Joaquín Truyol remaining at the helm.[3](#footnote-3)

• **[Phenna Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/phenna-group/posts/)** has welcomed **[Legitec](https://www.linkedin.com/company/legitec-legislacion-y-tecnologia/home/)**, a Spanish specialist in regulatory compliance, data protection and cybersecurity, founded in 2002 and based in the Murcia and Alicante region. Legitec supports clients with GDPR compliance, outsourced Data Protection Officer services, audits, whistleblowing arrangements, information security standards such as ISO 27001 and Spain’s ENS, NIS2 requirements, and technical security assessments. Its acquisition strengthens Phenna Group’s testing, inspection, certification and compliance capabilities in the digital environment.[4](#footnote-4)

• [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aperitivos-y-extrusionados-s-a-/posts/)**[Cinven](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cinven/posts/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cinven/posts/)has agreed to sell its stake in **[Restaurant Brands Europe (RBE)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/restaurant-brand-europe-sl/home/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/restaurant-brand-europe-sl/home/)to a consortium led by RBE founder Gregorio Jiménez, who will reinvest. Since Cinven invested in 2021, RBE has more than doubled EBITDA and expanded its restaurant network, launched Popeyes in Spain and Italy, and invested in technology, digital services, delivery, and loyalty. RBE operates more than 1,400 company-owned and franchised restaurants across Spain, Portugal, and Italy, featuring Burger King, Popeyes, and Tim Hortons in selected markets. Cinven said the business is now a scalable, technology-enabled platform and a leading QSR group in Iberia. [5](#footnote-5)

• **[Bechester](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bechester/home/)**, France’s **[Vestalis](https://www.linkedin.com/company/vestalis-fr/home/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/vestalis-fr/home/)and **[Move&Rent](https://www.linkedin.com/company/move-and-rent/home/)** have completed a strategic merger, forming a European group focused on furniture and equipment for real estate businesses. Operating across France, Spain and Portugal, the group will serve sectors including flexible living, coliving, student and senior residences, hospitality and corporate housing. It expects revenue of nearly €14 million in 2026 and aims to reach €25 million in 2027. The combined platform offers comprehensive furnishing through direct purchase or rental, allowing clients to spread costs over monthly payments and reduce upfront investment. Vestalis equips more than 4,000 rooms annually, while Bechester has completed over 3,500 projects and furnished more than 10,000 rooms since 2021. [6](#footnote-6)

• [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/optiogroup/home/)**[Mutares](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mutares/posts/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mutares/posts/)has agreed to sell Spanish energy-engineering company **[Guascor](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mutares/posts/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mutares/posts/)to **[Oaktree](https://www.linkedin.com/company/oaktree-capital-management/posts/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/oaktree-capital-management/posts/)Capital Management for €74 million, with the deal expected to close before the end of the fourth quarter, subject to customary approvals. Mutares acquired Guascor more than four years ago from Siemens Energy and repositioned it by restoring its original brand, improving efficiency, strengthening after-sales sales, and deepening direct customer relationships. Guascor recently acquired supplier Enertech and expects record EBITDA of €10 million this year, with more than double that projected for next year. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Applus+ Laboratories](https://www.linkedin.com/company/applus-laboratories/home/)** has acquired **[KAS Quality Service](https://www.linkedin.com/company/kastesting/)**, a fire testing laboratory founded in 2018 and based in Jiangmen, China. The acquisition strengthens Applus+’s construction product testing capabilities, including fire resistance, reaction-to-fire, smoke leakage, durability, and performance testing, while expanding its presence in South China. KAS contributes technical expertise, established customer relationships, and plans to grow its facilities and testing capabilities. [8](#footnote-8)

• [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/indragroupglobal/posts/)**[Boluda Shipping](https://www.linkedin.com/company/boludashipping/posts/)** has agreed to acquire Matrans Terminal in Rotterdam, strengthening its Northern European presence and expanding its port and logistics services. Located in Eemhaven, the 360,000 m² multipurpose terminal handles containers, forest products, metals, general and project cargo, and ro-ro traffic. It includes covered warehousing, quay and rail facilities, plus customs, transport and freight-forwarding services. The acquisition complements Boluda’s planned acquisition of Samskip; both deals remain subject to regulatory approval and closing conditions. Once completed, Boluda’s network will include 21 maritime terminals and five rail terminals, supporting better-connected intermodal logistics and more efficient supply chains. [9](#footnote-9)

• **[Boluda Towage](https://www.linkedin.com/company/boludatowage/home/)** has entered Ecuador by acquiring local tug operator Cititug, pending approval from competition authorities. Based in Guayaquil, Cititug mainly serves the Balao oil terminal, a key hydrocarbon shipping facility. The deal expands Boluda’s presence in Latin America and along the Pacific coast, following its entry into Honduras. It is part of a broader international growth strategy that includes recent acquisitions in the Netherlands and planned purchases in maritime transport. Boluda Towage operates more than 850 tugs across 232 ports.[10](#footnote-10)
