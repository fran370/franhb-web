• **[Marguerite](https://www.linkedin.com/company/marguerite-adviser/?originalSubdomain=lu)**, a Luxembourg-based firm, acquired the solar self-consumption group **[SotySolar](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sotysolar/?originalSubdomain=es)** from **Plenium Partners**, a Spanish fund specializing in renewable energies.[1](#footnote-1)

• **[Eranovum](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eranovum-energy/?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eranovum-energy/?originalSubdomain=es)acquired **Yeswecharge**, a sustainable mobility startup with rapid charging points in Poitiers, France. Additionally, Eranovum formed a joint venture with Belgian company **Ether Energy**. [2](#footnote-2)

• **[Nexxus Iberia](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nexxus-iberia/?originalSubdomain=es)**, through its subsidiary **[Twentyfour Seven Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/twentyfourseven-tv/)**, acquired a majority stake in **[Lee Films](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lee-films-international/?originalSubdomain=es)**, a Spanish advertising film production company. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[FCC Servicios Medio Ambiente](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fcc-medio-ambiente/?originalSubdomain=es)** agreed to buy the UK business of **[Urbaser](https://www.linkedin.com/company/urbaser/?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/urbaser/?originalSubdomain=es)for an estimated enterprise value of 398 million pounds (approximately €464 million). [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Gate Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/gategroup/?originalSubdomain=ch)**, the Swiss catering giant, presented a binding offer to acquire the majority of **[Nupik](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nupik/)**'s business, a Catalan manufacturer of disposable tableware. [5](#footnote-5)

• **[Gestcompost](https://www.linkedin.com/company/gestcompost/?originalSubdomain=es)** (**[Suma Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/suma-capital/?originalSubdomain=es)**), a leader in the treatment and energy recovery of organic and biodegradable waste in Spain, acquired a 60% stake in **Ecobiogás**, a Catalan engineering company specialized in biogas plants. [6](#footnote-6)

• Pablo Garriga, founder of **Collins Capital**, has acquired 100% of the capital of **Catamar**, marking another acquisition in the fishing sector. Last year, Garriga had acquired Maresmar, which already included two other Barcelona-based companies, Mariscos Laumar and Juan Roqueta e Hijos. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Brookfield](https://www.linkedin.com/company/brookfield-asset-management/)** reinforced **[HomeServe](https://www.linkedin.com/company/homeserve_es/?originalSubdomain=es)**'s business in Spain by acquiring **Rime Energía**, a company specializing in electricity and natural gas connections. [8](#footnote-8)

• The Peruvian multinational **[Grupo Virú](https://www.linkedin.com/company/viru-sa/?originalSubdomain=es)**, operating in the food sector, has strengthened its global operations by acquiring **[Fridela](https://www.linkedin.com/company/precocinados-fridela/about/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/precocinados-fridela/about/)and **Congelats Camós** in Spain. Both companies are focused on the prepared meals market​. [9](#footnote-9)

• **[Adapta Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/adapta-capital/?originalSubdomain=es)** has increased its stake in the Spanish laser hair removal group **[Elha](https://www.linkedin.com/company/centros-elha/?originalSubdomain=es)**, now owning 90% of the company. [10](#footnote-10)
