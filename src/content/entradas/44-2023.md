• **[Accenture](https://www.linkedin.com/company/accenture/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/accenture/)Acquires **[Innotec Security](https://www.linkedin.com/company/entelgy-innotec-security/)**, Spain-Based Leading Cybersecurity Company. Founded in 2002, Innotec Security was previously owned by parent company Entelgy Group. The acquisition will add 500 cybersecurity professionals to Accenture Security’s workforce. [1](#footnote-1)

• **[Oakley Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/oakley-capital-limited/)** has agreed to acquire a majority stake in **[Alerce](https://www.linkedin.com/company/alerce/)**, a leading Spanish provider of transport and logistics software solutions. Oakley will invest alongside Alerce’s founding family, including CEO Pablo Pardo Garcia, who will retain a significant stake in the business. [2](#footnote-2)

• The Spanish IT consulting firm **[Seresco](https://www.linkedin.com/company/seresco/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/seresco/)has acquired 75% of the Asturian company specialized in cybersecurity and regulatory compliance **[CIES](https://www.linkedin.com/company/instituto-cies/about/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/instituto-cies/about/)for an amount of 3.375 million euros. The turnover of at the end of 2022 reached 3.3 million. [3](#footnote-3)

• Spanish PE **[Quarza Inversiones](https://www.linkedin.com/company/quarza-inversiones/)** has joined forces with **[The Riverside Company](https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-riverside-company/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-riverside-company/)(Riverside) to acquire the German **[Dastex Reinraumzubehör](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dastex-reinraumzubeh%C3%B6r-gmbh&co-kg/)** (Dastex), one of the leading independent European distributors of textile garments and cleanroom consumables. [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Zegona](https://www.linkedin.com/company/vodafone/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/vodafone/)to acquire **[Vodafone](https://www.linkedin.com/company/vodafone/) Spain** for €5.0 billion. This acquisition is Zegona’s third deal in Spain after successful turnarounds at Telecable and Euskaltel. [5](#footnote-5)

• **[Zunibal](https://www.linkedin.com/company/zunibal/)**, portfolio company of **[Nazca Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/zunibal/),** acquires a minority stake in **[Cambrian Intelligence](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cambrian-intelligence/)** to consolidate its position as a world leader in technology for sustainable fisheries. [6](#footnote-6)

• **[Nexus Energía](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nexusenergia/)**, the eighth electricity and gas marketer and the second representative of renewable producers in Spain, has closed an agreement to acquire 20% of the capital of the Valencian company **Elekluz**. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Bluevía](https://www.linkedin.com/company/bluevia/)**, the wholesale network services company, has acquired a package of fiber assets from the local telecommunications operator **[Excom](https://www.linkedin.com/company/free-technologies-excom/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/free-technologies-excom/)in Andalucia. Bluevía is a company of which Telefónica controls 55%, while the remaining 45% belongs to Crédit Agricole and Vauban Infrastructure Partners**.** [8](#footnote-8)

• **[Substrate AI](https://www.linkedin.com/company/substrateai/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/substrateai/)has formalized the acquisition of 70% of **[Binit](https://www.linkedin.com/company/binit-tech/)**, software development firm based in Spain, the United Kingdom and Latin America. [9](#footnote-9)

• **[GPF Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/gpfcapital/)** acquired a minority stake in Mexican consumer debt management company **[Resuelve Tu Deuda](https://www.linkedin.com/company/go-bravo-resuelve-tu-deuda-global/)**. [10](#footnote-10)
