• Italian Private Equity firm **[Charme Capital Partners](https://www.linkedin.com/company/charme-capital-partners-limited/)** has acquired a minority stake in **[Universae](https://www.linkedin.com/company/universae/)**, a Spanish regulated vocational training (FP) group, investing around 30 million euros to boost its international expansion, particularly in Latin America**.** [1](#footnote-1)

• **[Nazca](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nazcacapital/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nazcacapital/)launches its Small Cap II fund and strengthens its commitment to sustainability with an investment in **[Global](https://www.linkedin.com/company/global-factor/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/global-factor/)**[Factor](https://www.linkedin.com/company/global-factor/)**, a leader in decarbonisation services. Global Factor closed 2023 with revenues of €14 million, with a strong international presence (c. 50% of sales) and annual growth of over 40% in recent years, thanks to its unique positioning in a growing sector such as sustainability. [2](#footnote-2)

• Navarre-based business group **[Amixalán](https://www.linkedin.com/company/amixalan/)**, founded by Xabier Blanco, has finalized the acquisition of an 85% majority stake in the company **[3D](https://www.linkedin.com/company/3dmodular/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/3dmodular/)**[Modular](https://www.linkedin.com/company/3dmodular/)**, thus entering the industrialized modular construction sector. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[ILUNION](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ilunion/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ilunion/)has finalized the agreement to acquire 80% of **Grupo** **Recicab**, a company specializing in waste recovery and valorization. With over 30 years of experience, Recicab offers value-added services related to the recovery and valorization of various types of hazardous and non-hazardous waste, particularly focusing on paper and cardboard recovery along with associated services. Currently operating four production centers in Cádiz, Málaga, Córdoba, and Almería, Recicab recovered over 100,000 tons of paper and cardboard in 2023 and employs 37 people. [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Providence](https://www.linkedin.com/company/providence-equity-partners/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/providence-equity-partners/)**[Equity](https://www.linkedin.com/company/providence-equity-partners/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/providence-equity-partners/)**[Partners](https://www.linkedin.com/company/providence-equity-partners/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/providence-equity-partners/)makes its entry into Spain's fitness sector by acquiring **[VivaGym](https://www.linkedin.com/company/vivagym-group/)**, the leading independent low-cost gym chain in the country, with 104 centers across the Iberian Peninsula. This move follows Basic Fit's recent acquisition of RSG Group's business in Spain, marking a significant shift in the low-cost gym industry. VivaGym has nearly recovered pre-pandemic revenue, reaching €93 million in 2023. Providence aims for a 25% annual growth rate.[5](#footnote-5)

• The French group **[Paprec](https://www.linkedin.com/company/paprec/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/paprec/)has acquired the Valencian company **[Guerola](https://www.linkedin.com/company/reciclatges-guerola-sl/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/reciclatges-guerola-sl/)to enter the paper recycling sector in Spain. This acquisition follows Paprec's recent purchase of Recimed, a waste management company based in Catalonia, emphasizing its strategic expansion in Spain's recycling industry. Guerola's specialization in paper recycling complements Paprec's portfolio. [6](#footnote-6)

• **[Knowmad](https://www.linkedin.com/company/knowmad-mood/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/knowmad-mood/)**[Mood](https://www.linkedin.com/company/knowmad-mood/)**, a technology consulting firm specializing in digital transformation solutions, announced the acquisition of the German consulting and software development company **[ASERVO](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aservo-ag/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aservo-ag/)**[Software](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aservo-ag/)**. With this purchase, the Spanish consulting firm enters the German market, a market with high potential, and strengthens its positioning in ALM and DevO. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Axel Johnson International](https://www.linkedin.com/company/axel-johnson-international/)** Acquires a Majority Stake in **[Royse](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rodamientos-y-servicios-sl/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/rodamientos-y-servicios-sl/)for Expansion in the Spanish Market. Royse, a family-owned company based in Seville, Spain, operates 14 branches, employs over 160 people nationwide and is the largest independently owned company in the Iberian power transmission market. [8](#footnote-8)

• Rotterdam-based group **[Caldic](https://www.linkedin.com/company/caldic/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/caldic/)incorporates the assets of family-owned specialty solutions distributor **[Ricardo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ricardomolina/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ricardomolina/)**[Molina](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ricardomolina/)** to boost its business in Spain and South America. The Dutch company acquires Catalonia-based Ricardo Molina, specializing in distributing special ingredients and chemicals for industries, food, and pharmaceuticals. [9](#footnote-9)

• North American multinational **[Lear](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lear-corporation/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lear-corporation/)**[Corporation](https://www.linkedin.com/company/lear-corporation/)**, with plants in Porriño and Vigo, has recently announced the acquisition of another company in Spain. This time, it is **[WIP](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wip-proyectos-industriales/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wip-proyectos-industriales/)**[Industrial](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wip-proyectos-industriales/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wip-proyectos-industriales/)**[Automation](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wip-proyectos-industriales/)**, a firm located in Valladolid and specialized in automation. [10](#footnote-10)
