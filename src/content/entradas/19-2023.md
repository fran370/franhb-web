• **[H2B2](https://www.linkedin.com/company/h2b2/)** saltará al Nasdaq tras el acuerdo de fusión con la SPAC de EEUU **RMG III**. [1](#footnote-1)

• **[Sener](https://agqlabs.es/2023/05/08/netpharmalab-se-integra-en-el-grupo-agq-labs/)** [](https://agqlabs.es/2023/05/08/netpharmalab-se-integra-en-el-grupo-agq-labs/)se refuerza en el mundo del dato con la compra de **[Quark](https://www.linkedin.com/company/quark-technological-solutions)**. [2](#footnote-2)

• **[Garrigues](https://www.linkedin.com/company/garrigues/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/garrigues/)adquiere el 51% de **[EAD Trust](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eadtrust/).** [3](#footnote-3)

• **[Nexxus Iberia](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nexxus-iberia)** vende el líder de radioterapia en Portugal al fondo británico **[iCON Infrastructure](https://www.linkedin.com/company/icon-infrastructure-llp/).** [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Intrum](https://www.linkedin.com/company/intrum/)** acquires servicing platform **[Haya Real Estate](https://www.linkedin.com/company/haya-real-estate)**, reinforcing Intrum's position in Servicing and Spain.[5](#footnote-5)

• **[Kartesia](https://www.linkedin.com/company/kartesia/)** announced that it has sold the Spanish bioingredients company **[ADL Biopharma](https://www.linkedin.com/company/adlbiopharma)** to **[Wacker Chemie AG](https://www.linkedin.com/company/wacker/)**, a multinational chemical group. [6](#footnote-6)

• **[EiDF Solar](https://www.linkedin.com/company/eidfsolar/?originalSubdomain=es)** compra al **[Banco Sabadell](https://www.linkedin.com/company/banco-sabadell/?originalSubdomain=es)** las instalaciones fotovoltaicas donde operaban conjuntamente. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Amixalan](https://www.linkedin.com/company/amixalan/?originalSubdomain=es)**, el nuevo grupo del expropietario de STI Norland, compra la mayoría de **[Ega Perfil](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ega-perfil/?original_referer=https%3A%2F%2Fwww%2Egoogle%2Ecom%2F&originalSubdomain=es).** [8](#footnote-8)

• **[TotalEnergies](https://www.linkedin.com/company/totalenergies/)** Expands Activities in Europe by Acquiring **[Iber Resinas](https://www.linkedin.com/company/iber-resinas/?originalSubdomain=es).** [9](#footnote-9)

• **[AGQ Labs](https://www.linkedin.com/company/agq-labs/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/agq-labs/)adquiere una participación mayoritaria en **[Netpharmalab](https://www.linkedin.com/company/netpharmalab-consulting-services/?originalSubdomain=es)**. [10](#footnote-10)
