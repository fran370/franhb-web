• **[Kartesia](https://www.linkedin.com/company/kartesia/)**, European specialist provider of financing solutions, announced that it has provided a financing facility to support the buy-and-build acquisition strategy of the leading property management business **[Portik Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/portik-property-management/)**. [1](#footnote-1)

• **[CVS Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/cvsgroup/) ([Ufenau Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ufenau-capital-partners-ag/))**, which invested in R&M, the specialist in installation and maintenance of refrigeration plants in 2021, has acquired two companies with a solid track record in the technical installations and maintenance sector, **[Proteknia Fire](https://www.linkedin.com/company/proteknia/)** and **[Ebrofrío](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ebrofrio/)**. [2](#footnote-2)

• **[Clar](https://www.linkedin.com/company/clar/)**, a global digital loan broker based in Sweden, has acquired the Barcelona-born startup **[Finteca](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fintecatech/) ([Nuclio](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nuclio/))**, which will become part of **[Prestalo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/prestalocom/)**, Clar's loan broker in Spain. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[Abante](https://www.linkedin.com/company/abante-asesores/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/abante-asesores/)acquires **[Welzia](https://www.linkedin.com/company/welzia-management-sgiic/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/welzia-management-sgiic/)and adds 1,000 million assets in its fourth acquisition in three years. [4](#footnote-4)

• **Volati** has acquired all shares in Silos Metálicos Zaragoza S.L.U. (**[SIMEZA](https://www.linkedin.com/company/simeza-silos/)**), a leading European manufacturer of storage solutions for the grain industry. The acquisition of SIMEZA is an add-on acquisition for **[Tornum Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tornum/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tornum/)and is the platform's fifth acquisition in three years.[5](#footnote-5)

• **[Atrys Health](https://www.linkedin.com/company/atrys-health/)**, a global healthtech company that provides prevention, diagnosis and precision medical treatment services, a pioneer in telemedicine and oncology treatment, announces that it has reached an agreement for the sale of 75.1% of **[Conversia](https://www.linkedin.com/company/conversia-protecci-n-de-datos-s-l-/)** for an amount of 35 million euros to the investment group **Arraigo Midco**.[6](#footnote-6)

• **[Pañalon Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/panalon-s.a./)** acquires **Transportes Félix Group**. In less than half a year, Pañalon has announced the acquisition of three companies dedicated to food transport in tankers in Spain. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Sony Music](https://www.linkedin.com/company/sony-music-entertainment/)** reaches an agreement to acquire **[Altafonte Network](https://www.linkedin.com/company/altafonte/)**, one of the main independent record distributors in Spain and Latin America. [8](#footnote-8)

• **[Almawave](https://www.linkedin.com/company/almawave/)**, an Italian company offering AI-powered language-recognition solutions, announced the acquisition of a 70% stake in Spain-based **[Mabrian](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mabrian/)**. [9](#footnote-9)

• **[Purever](https://www.linkedin.com/company/purever-group/)**, a Portugal-based company specializing in thermal insulation solutions, acquired Spain-based **[Taver](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupotaverinstaclack/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupotaverinstaclack/)and **[Instaclack Internacional](https://www.linkedin.com/company/grupotaverinstaclack/)**. Both companies, which are part of the same group, are based in Catalonia and specialize in the manufacturing of inssulating panels. [10](#footnote-10)
