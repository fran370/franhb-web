> Merry Christmas! 🎅

• **[The Workout Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/workout-events/?originalSubdomain=es)**, which includes Workout Events (event services and staffing), Workout Retail (focused on point-of-sale implementation and promotion), and Workout ETT (specializing in talent selection), has added **[Goros Investments](https://www.linkedin.com/company/goros-investments)** as a new partner to its project​.[1](#footnote-1)

• **[Surexport](https://www.linkedin.com/company/surexport/?originalSubdomain=es)**, a leading European player in the production and commercialization of berries, has acquired a majority stake in **[Jong](https://www.linkedin.com/company/jong-fruit-b.v./)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/jong-fruit-b.v./)**[Fruit](https://www.linkedin.com/company/jong-fruit-b.v./)**, a Dutch strawberry producer. [2](#footnote-2)

• **[Innometal](https://www.linkedin.com/company/imar-innometal/?originalSubdomain=es)** (**[Mirai](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mirai-investments/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mirai-investments/)**[Investments](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mirai-investments/))** has acquired Catalan manufacturer of metal structures **[Manipulados y Acabados Metálicos (MAM)](https://www.linkedin.com/company/manipulados-y-acabados-metalicos/?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/manipulados-y-acabados-metalicos/?originalSubdomain=es)according to La Vanguardia. Founded in 1978, MAM employs 220 people, has two plants in Catalonia and had a turnover of EUR 36m in 2022. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[Ferrovial](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ferrovial/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ferrovial/)and **[Renault Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/renaultgroup)** have reached an agreement to sell the entire 50% of Ferrovial’s stake in **[Zity by Mobilize](https://www.linkedin.com/company/zity-mobility/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/zity-mobility/)to its partner Renault Group. Via Mobilize, Renault Group has decided to pursue its initiatives in terms of responsible and easy-to-access mobility solutions while Ferrovial will continue to focus its investments on activities linked to its core business, the development and operation of sustainable infrastructure. [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Basic-Fit](https://www.linkedin.com/company/basic-fit/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/basic-fit/)has reached an agreement with the **[RSG Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/basic-fit/)**[,](https://www.linkedin.com/company/basic-fit/) one of the largest multi-brand fitness club operators in Europe, for the acquisition of RSG Spain. With the intended acquisition, Basic-Fit will acquire all 42 **McFIT** clubs and all 5 **Holmes Place** clubs in Spain. [5](#footnote-5)

• “**[MaserGrup](https://www.linkedin.com/company/serveis-i-administracions-masergrup-sl/?originalSubdomain=es) – [Fruselva](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fruselva/)**”, Spanish group with productive activity in the food and beverage sectors acquires the food holding group “**[Costa Concentrados Levantinos](https://www.linkedin.com/company/costa-concentrados-levantinos-sl/?originalSubdomain=es)**”. Costa Concentrados Levantinos is a company with Valencian origins that emerged from the Costa family’s venture in 1897 in Albal (Valencia) with the aim of creating a unique tiger nut concentrate. [6](#footnote-6)

• **[ProA](https://www.linkedin.com/company/proa-capital/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/proa-capital/)**[Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/proa-capital/)**, a Spanish private equity firm, has acquired a majority stake (around 70%) in **[Acrylicos](https://www.linkedin.com/company/acrylicos-vallejo/?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/acrylicos-vallejo/?originalSubdomain=es)**[Vallejo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/acrylicos-vallejo/?originalSubdomain=es)**, a manufacturer of acrylic paints for model-making, fine arts, and decorative arts. [7](#footnote-7)

• India-based online travel booking portal [TBO](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tbo-com/?originalSubdomain=in) **Tek**'s subsidiary, Tek Travels DMCC, has acquired 100% shareholding of **Jumbonline Accommodations & Services**, which has been demerged as an online business from Spain-based **[Jumbo Tours Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/jumbo-tours-group/?originalSubdomain=es)**, one of the biggest international tourism operators. With this acquisition, TBO intends to further increase its presence in Europe. [8](#footnote-8)

• **[Seidor Tech](https://www.linkedin.com/company/seidortech/?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/seidortech/?originalSubdomain=es)has acquired Valladolid-based **[Avenet IT](https://www.linkedin.com/company/avenet-it/?originalSubdomain=es)**. This acquisition expands Seidor Tech's geographical reach into Castilla y León and is expected to increase the company's annual turnover to €30 million and its workforce to over 200 employees. Avenet IT will contribute more than 20 employees, €7 million in annual turnover and an expanded portfolio of technological services and solutions. This purchase follows Seidor Tech's earlier acquisitions of Valnera Consultoría y Sistemas and Avanti21. [9](#footnote-9)

• The private equity firm **Nzyme** (backed by **[Kibo Ventures](https://www.linkedin.com/company/kiboventures/)** and supported by [Oliver Wyman](https://www.linkedin.com/company/oliver-wyman/) and **[Almond](https://www.linkedin.com/company/almond-capital/)**) acquired a majority stake in **[Dental Ibérica](https://www.linkedin.com/company/dental-iberica/)**, a dental materials and products distributor based in Asturias, Spain. The acquisition aims to support the company's growth both in Spain and internationally. [10](#footnote-10)
