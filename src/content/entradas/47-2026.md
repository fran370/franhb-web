• **[Avanade](https://www.linkedin.com/company/avanade/home/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/avanade/home/)has agreed to acquire **[Plain Concepts](https://www.linkedin.com/company/plainconcepts/posts/) ([ABE Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/abe-capital-partners/posts/))**, a Spain-headquartered IT consultancy with more than 600 professionals specializing in Microsoft services, software engineering, AI, data and cloud modernization. The acquisition is expected to expand Avanade’s capabilities and customer base across Spain and Europe, strengthening its presence in Barcelona and Madrid and extending its reach into Bilbao and Porto. Plain Concepts will gain access to Avanade’s Microsoft expertise and Accenture’s industry knowledge and global reach.[1](#footnote-1)

• **[Talde Private Equity](https://www.linkedin.com/company/talde-gesti-n-s.g.e.c.r.-s.a./posts/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/talde-gesti-n-s.g.e.c.r.-s.a./posts/)has led a group of investors in acquiring 100% of **[Elgorriaga Brands](https://www.linkedin.com/company/elgorriaga-brands-sa?originalSubdomain=es)**, a historic Spanish biscuit and chocolate maker founded in Irún in 1770. The investor group includes entrepreneur Santiago Sala, Mendibea, Eguia, Peter De Haan’s family office, and Fundación Caja de Burgos. Now based in Ávila, Elgorriaga generates 70% of its sales abroad, particularly in Mexico and Brazil. The company has grown by an average of 13% annually since 2021 and recorded €13.7 million in revenue in 2025. [2](#footnote-2)

• **[Grupo Tello Alimentación](https://www.linkedin.com/company/tello-alimentacion/home/)** has acquired Moreno Plaza, a Málaga-based meat company with €55 million in revenue and 157 employees. The acquisition will bring the group’s combined revenue to €465 million and its workforce to 1,600, strengthening its position among Spain’s ten largest meat-sector operators and expanding its fresh-meat business. Moreno Plaza, a family business with more than a century of history, will continue operating with its existing activity and employment structure. Tello says the deal supports its growth, broadens its product range, and strengthens its industrial presence in southern Spain. [3](#footnote-3)

• **[knowmad mood](https://www.linkedin.com/company/knowmad-mood/posts/)** acquired 100% of **[Danwor](https://www.linkedin.com/company/danwor/home/)**, an Argentina-based Data & Analytics, business planning, and applied AI consultancy founded in 2015. Danwor brings more than 70 professionals, around 40 international clients, and expertise in SAP, Databricks, and Google Cloud. Its founders will continue leading the company. The deal strengthens knowmad mood’s Data & AI and SAP capabilities, expands its presence in Latin America, and creates opportunities for joint projects and cross-selling. [4](#footnote-4)

• **[Grupo Fertiberia](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fertiberia-s-a/posts/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/fertiberia-s-a/posts/)has completed the integration of **[Codisa-Dicaher](https://www.linkedin.com/company/codisa-dicaher-sl/home/)**, a Valencia-based company with over 35 years of expertise in fertigation for high-value crops. The acquisition adds a liquid fertiliser production plant and an experienced technical team, strengthening Fertiberia’s offering in eastern Spain and supporting growth in specialised, high-value agriculture. [5](#footnote-5)

• Private equity firm **[Aurica Capital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/aurica-capital/posts/)** has acquired a ~70% majority stake in **[Axon Partners Group Consulting](https://www.linkedin.com/company/axon-partners-group/posts/)**, valuing the division at €35 million plus a conditional €5 million earn-out in 2028. Founded in Madrid in 2012, the unit specializes in cybersecurity, regulatory, and strategic advisory for governments and corporations, reaching nearly €16 million in 2025 revenue. The deal excludes Axon’s investment management arm and includes significant management reinvestment to drive organic and inorganic growth. [6](#footnote-6)

• **[Mirai](https://www.linkedin.com/company/mirai-investments/posts/)**, the group founded by José Antonio Jainaga and former Sidenor executives, has acquired **[Polypal Storage Systems](https://www.linkedin.com/company/polypal?originalSubdomain=es)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/polypal?originalSubdomain=es)through its subsidiary **[Ohmnia](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ohmnia-group/posts/)**. Based in Iurreta, Polypal makes industrial storage systems and brings Ohmnia a presence in a new sector, international sales subsidiaries, and operations in around 30 countries. The company employs 190 people, serves 1,600 customers, and generates more than €70 million in annual revenue. Ohmnia plans to remain a long-term shareholder and expand Polypal’s capacity. The acquisition adds to Ohmnia’s portfolio of 19 companies, whose combined revenue reached €165 million in 2025. Polypal was previously owned by Whittan, backed by EMK Capital and Bregal Investments. [7](#footnote-7)

• **[Woolpert](https://www.linkedin.com/company/woolpert/home/)** has acquired Barcelona-based **[B-Global Tech](https://www.linkedin.com/company/b-global-tech/home/)**, expanding its global data center and mission-critical architecture and engineering capabilities across Europe, Africa, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific. Founded in 2001, B-Global Tech has more than 200 professionals in Spain and Southeast Asia, bringing expertise in hyperscale and AI data centers, direct liquid cooling, and complex digital infrastructure. Its work includes a facility for an EU flagship supercomputer, contributions to Spain’s EU AI gigafactory, and Asia-Pacific’s first NVIDIA data center. The acquisition gives Woolpert more than 1,200 professionals focused on data center infrastructure and strengthens its ability to deliver complex projects responsibly, with expertise spanning multidisciplinary design, MEP, geospatial, commissioning, and operational readiness. [8](#footnote-8)

• [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/indragroupglobal/posts/)**[Namirial](https://www.linkedin.com/company/namirial/posts/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/namirial/posts/)has acquired Barcelona-based **[Send2Sign](https://www.linkedin.com/company/qualitahub/home/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/qualitahub/home/)to expand its electronic-signature offering and strengthen its presence in Spain. Send2Sign provides cloud-based electronic-signature and document-workflow solutions, including remote, biometric, centralised and automated signing, with integrations for systems such as SAP, Oracle and Microsoft. The acquisition builds on a long-standing partnership between Send2Sign and Validated ID, which is part of the Namirial Group. Namirial CEO Massimiliano Pellegrini described the deal as a natural evolution of that partnership and welcomed the Send2Sign team. Namirial provides digital trust, identity and transaction-management services in more than 90 countries. [9](#footnote-9)

• French homeopathy leader **[Boiron](https://www.linkedin.com/company/laboratoire-boiron/home/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/laboratoire-boiron/home/)has acquired **[Hivital](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hivital/home/)** [](https://www.linkedin.com/company/hivital/home/)Labs, a Catalan firm specializing in online dietary supplements. Founded in 2018 by Ferran Forcada, Hivital markets roughly 70 products across Spain, Germany, Italy, and France, generating €13 million in revenue and nearly €3 million in profit in 2025. The undisclosed deal allows Boiron, which posts over €500 million in annual revenue, to expand its portfolio and digital capabilities. In return, Hivital gains access to Boiron’s global distribution network, logistics, and pharmaceutical regulatory expertise. Hivital will retain its brand, autonomy, and 10-person team, with Forcada remaining general manager under Boiron CEO Pascal Houdayer.[10](#footnote-10)
